Research Background

How do international TikTok creators portray Shanghai?

This Digital Humanities project investigates how overseas TikTok creators experience Shanghai through travel videos. Rather than analysing official media, the project focuses on spontaneous urban encounters, revealing how global visitors construct Shanghai's city image through movement, food, architecture and everyday interaction.

Key Insights

Urban Image

International creators emphasise modernity, accessibility and vibrant everyday experiences rather than traditional sightseeing.

Experience

Food and walkable public spaces generate the strongest emotional responses, becoming important symbols of Shanghai.

Communication

User-generated videos provide alternative narratives beyond official city branding, revealing authentic global perceptions.
