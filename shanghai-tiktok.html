Research Overview

How do international TikTok creators construct Shanghai's urban image?

As global tourism continues to recover after the pandemic, a new wave of international travellers has turned everyday journeys into widely shared digital stories. The rise of the China Travel phenomenon on TikTok has transformed personal experiences into public narratives, allowing millions of viewers to encounter Chinese cities through creators' eyes. Among these destinations, Shanghai stands out as a globally connected metropolis where historical heritage, contemporary lifestyles, and urban innovation intersect. This project investigates how creators experience, interpret, and communicate Shanghai through embodied interactions with urban space. By combining qualitative content analysis with Digital Humanities visualization, the dashboard reveals spatial patterns, emotional responses, and emerging representations that together shape Shanghai's contemporary international image.
