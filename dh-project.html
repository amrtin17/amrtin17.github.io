Re-visualising the City

Thesis project · UCC Digital Arts & Humanities

This final thesis project for UCC's Digital Arts & Humanities programme asks how city promotional videos construct a place, and how those constructions can be re-visualised for cultural analysis.

The practical outcome is an interactive dashboard examining how international TikTok creators represent Shanghai: the places they film, the activities they foreground, and the emotional tone of their videos. It combines close reading with data visualisation to make patterns visible without losing the texture of individual videos.

The dashboard was built with D3.js, ECharts, and Leaflet. The dataset was collected and coded by hand, with each video treated as both a data point and a piece of visual culture.

Project notes

  • Research question: how global creators construct Shanghai through short-form video
  • Methods: close reading, manual coding, and interactive data visualisation
  • Tools: D3.js, ECharts, Leaflet, and hand-built datasets
